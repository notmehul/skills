# Indian regulation a builder must respect: in force versus draft

Status as of 17 Oct 2026. Re-check the regulator's page before quoting a date or a status; drafts finalise and effective dates slip.

| Instrument | Status | What it means for a builder |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025 (8 May 2025) | In force. Multi-lender comparison (para 6) from 1 Nov 2025; app reporting from 15 Jun 2025 | A lending service provider's conduct is the lender's liability. The provider keeps only name, address and contact. Need-based data with explicit documented consent; no access to contacts, files or call logs; one-time camera, mic and location for KYC only. Unbiased offer comparison with APR, EMI, penal charges and KFS links, unmatched lenders named, ranking metric public, no dark patterns. Cooling-off of at least one day. Recovery-agent details before contact. Data stored in India; offshore processing deleted within 24 hours |
| Key Facts Statement circular (15 Apr 2024) | In force | APR, all third-party charges, no undisclosed fee. Any bot quoting a cost mirrors the KFS |
| RBI Pre-payment Charges on Loans Directions, 2025 (2 Jul 2025) | In force for loans sanctioned or renewed on or after 1 Jan 2026 | Floating-rate loans to individuals for non-business purposes: zero prepayment charge, no lock-in. Loans sanctioned earlier follow their agreement |
| FREE-AI committee report (13 Aug 2025) | Recommendations; RBI "assessing" per its annual report of May 2026 | 7 sutras and 26 recommendations: board AI policy, AI inventory, incident reporting, disclosure to consumers, a tolerant approach to low-risk AI |
| RBI draft Guidance on Regulatory Principles for Model Risk Management (24 Jun 2026; comments closed 24 Jul 2026) | Draft. Final not found as of 17 Oct 2026 (UNVERIFIED) | Applies to all models, including spreadsheets used for pricing. Board-approved framework; risk tiering reviewed annually; independent validation including third-party models; explainability thresholds; hallucination controls; prompt-injection defences; kill switch; human-in-command; customer disclosure and human fallback. Build to it now |
| RBI Responsible Business Conduct Directions 2025, NBFC amendment effective 1 Jul 2026 | In force | Defines compulsory bundling, dark patterns, explicit consent and mis-selling. An NBFC publishes its agent list, a code of conduct for agents, a suitability policy and compensation for mis-selling |
| KYC Master Direction (updated 14 Aug 2025; entity-wise re-issue 28 Nov 2025) | In force | Aadhaar face authentication allowed for e-KYC. Video KYC needs liveness, geotag and an audit trail; Aadhaar XML or QR at most 3 working days old. OTP e-KYC accounts limited to one year unless upgraded. No KYC rejection "without application of mind" |
| Account Aggregator framework (NBFC-AA Master Direction 2016); Sahamati recognised as SRO 5 Jun 2026 | In force. Interoperability and central-registry integration by 31 Dec 2026 (RBI, 7 Oct 2026); a new fee model proposed Oct 2026 | Consented, machine-readable financial data replaces statement uploads and SMS scraping. 595 million cumulative consents and 28.9 million a month as of Sep 2026 |
| DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 (notified 13 Nov 2025) | Phased. Board and definitions in force now; consent managers from 13 Nov 2026; notice, security, breach notification, data-principal rights and SDF duties from 13 May 2027 | Standalone itemised consent notices; withdrawal as easy as consent; 90-day response to rights requests; breach notice to the Board and to affected people, detailed report within 72 hours; penalties up to ₹250 crore. SDFs: annual DPIA and audit; an "algorithmic due diligence" duty is reported but UNVERIFIED in the final text. Build consent logging now |
| TRAI TCCCPR amendments (12 Feb 2025; Third Amendment 18 Sep 2026) and the 1600-series Direction (19 Nov 2025) | In force. 1600-series mandatory for banks from 1 Jan 2026, NBFCs above ₹5,000 crore from 1 Feb 2026, the rest from 1 Mar 2026 | Transactional means within 30 minutes of a customer-initiated transaction. Service calls on 1600, promotion on 140 only with registered digital consent. Promotional content inside a service call makes the call promotional. Legacy consents valid only if verifiable and registered on DLT. Abandoned-call limits on predictive dialers |
| IT Rules amendment on synthetically generated information (notified 10 Feb 2026, effective 20 Feb 2026); India AI Governance Guidelines (5 Nov 2025) | Rules binding on intermediaries; Guidelines voluntary | Prominent labels on synthetic audio and visual content, a prefixed disclosure on permitted synthetic audio; a grievance mechanism for AI harms. Whether a lender's own text-to-speech outbound call is covered is contested: use an audible "automated call" opener |
| Insurance cross-sell: Responsible Business Conduct Directions; IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders (5 Sep 2024); NHB direction to housing finance companies (Mar 2025); Department of Financial Services instruction (Jun 2025) | In force | No compulsory bundling of insurance with a home loan; the customer may buy from any insurer; housing finance companies take separate consent and offer at least two options; 30-day free look; signed benefit illustration; sales incentives de-linked. A loan bot never implies cover is required |
| Fair Practices Code (banks, NBFCs, housing finance companies); FACE debt-recovery guidelines (29 Aug 2025) | In force; FACE guidelines are the fintech SRO's reading | No discrimination on sex, caste or religion; transparent reasons for rejection on request. Recovery calls 08:00 to 19:00; AI agents identify themselves and disclose automation; all calls recorded and audited |
| SEBI Intermediaries (Amendment) Regulations 2025 (6 Feb 2025); consultation on responsible AI (20 Jun 2025) | Amendment in force: the regulated entity is "solely responsible" for AI outputs. No final circular from the consultation found as of Oct 2026 (UNVERIFIED) | Relevant only when the product is a security or investment, not a loan |
