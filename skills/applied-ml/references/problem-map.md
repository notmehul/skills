# Problem to method map

Defaults as of Oct 2026. "Default" is what to build first. "LLM instead" is the condition under which an LLM, zero-shot or as a labeller, beats a trained model. "Data" is the minimum before starting. Rung numbers refer to the baseline ladder in `SKILL.md`.

| Problem | Default | LLM instead when | Data you need |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Churn, retention | Binary classifier on a point-in-time feature snapshot. GBDT baseline; TabICLv2 or TabPFN as the second model under ~100K rows. Calibrate probabilities. Report lift at top-k, not AUC alone. | Never as the scorer. Label churn intent from tickets and call notes and feed it as a feature. | Customer-period table with features frozen at prediction time, an outcome window ("no purchase in the next 60 days"), a few hundred positives or more. |
| Lead scoring | Same shape as churn. Rank by calibrated probability times deal value. | Under ~200 labelled conversions: an LLM rubric on enrichment text as the interim scorer, replaced once outcomes exist. It cannot be trained against your AUC. | Lead attributes at creation time, outcome with timestamps, source. |
| Pricing, demand forecasting | Per-SKU or hierarchical: statsforecast baselines, then mlforecast + LightGBM with lags, calendar and promo covariates, then Chronos-2 zero-shot with covariates. Pick by rolling-origin backtest. Quantiles, not points, for inventory. | LLMs are not forecasters. Use one to parse promo calendars and events into covariates. | Date-grained history of at least two seasonal cycles, hierarchy keys, known-future covariates (price, promo, holidays). |
| Inventory | Quantile forecast, then a newsvendor reorder point (service level gives the quantile). Intermittent demand: Croston, TSB or ADIDA in statsforecast. | Not applicable. | Daily demand, lead times, stockout flags (censored demand is the usual leakage). |
| Anomaly, fraud | With labels: supervised GBDT with class weights, PR-AUC, cost-weighted threshold. Without: IsolationForest or ECOD (PyOD) first, then feed the anomaly score as a feature as labels accrue. | Triage explanations of flagged cases and extraction of unstructured signals; never the detector at volume. | Transaction table, confirmed labels (expect delay), the review budget. |
| Segmentation | RFM or behavioural features, then k-means or GMM on scaled features; choose k by business use, not silhouette alone. Validate segments against an outcome. | Naming and describing segments; clustering free text (embed, then cluster); persona summaries from survey text. | Behavioural aggregates per customer. |
| Recommendation | Popularity plus co-occurrence and item-item similarity first; implicit-feedback matrix factorisation, LightGCN or a two-tower past ~10K users with interaction logs; rerank with a GBDT on business features. | Cold start and long tail via item-text embeddings. LLM reranking only after a measured win: 2026 papers show LLM rerankers often demote items the collaborative model ranked correctly, at high cost. | Interaction log (user, item, timestamp, event type), catalogue text. |
| Uplift, causal ("did it work") | Randomised: A/B with CUPED and a stopping rule; S/T/X-learner with LightGBM for targeting, judged by Qini and AUUC on a randomised holdout. Observational: DoWhy identify, EconML estimate, refute. Geo: synthetic control. `references/experiments.md`. | Draft the DAG and surface forgotten confounders. Never to estimate the effect. | Treatment assignment with a known mechanism, pre-treatment covariates, outcome. Geo: 20 to 40 units with a year or more of pre-period. |
| Text and document classification, extraction | Fixed label set with ~500 or more labels: embeddings (Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B or bge-m3) plus logistic regression, or a fine-tuned small encoder. Extraction: Docling or OCR, then an LLM with a Pydantic schema, validated per field. | Few labels, drifting taxonomy, open-ended reasoning or low volume. Fine-tuned classifiers beat zero-shot LLMs when labels exist, and the gap widens with many classes (one 77-class test: ~94% against ~68%). | Labelled examples, a written label guide, document samples spanning every layout. |
| Search, ranking | Hybrid BM25 plus dense embeddings with reciprocal-rank fusion, then a cross-encoder reranker (bge-reranker-v2-m3 or Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B) on the top 50. Offline nDCG@10 on a small judged set. | As the judge that builds the relevance set cheaply; at query time only when latency and cost allow. | Corpus, query log, 100 to 300 judged (query, document) pairs. |
