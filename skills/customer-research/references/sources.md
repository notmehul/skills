# Public sources for customer research

Where to listen before you ask. Read the original page, not a search snippet, and record the date of everything you quote. People's words are evidence about their experience, not facts about the market. Use only what they posted in a work or public capacity.

## Search operators

```
site:reddit.com "<problem>" "recommend" OR "alternative" OR "switched"
site:reddit.com/r/<subreddit> "<keyword>"
site:news.ycombinator.com "<competitor or category>"
site:github.com "<competitor or library>" is:issue          (or search issues on GitHub directly)
site:linkedin.com/jobs "<role>" "<tool or problem>"
"<competitor>" -filter:replies min_faves:10                  (X search)
"<problem>" "anyone know" OR "recommend" OR "alternative"    (X search)
"<competitor 1> vs <competitor 2>"                            (read the comments)
```

## What each source is good for, and its bias

| Source | Good for | Bias |
|---|---|---|
| Interviews (unprompted answers) | Why, in their words | A small sample of engaged people |
| Win/loss or churn conversations | What tipped the decision | Memory fades and people rationalise |
| App store, G2 and other reviews | Recurring praise and complaints | Strong opinions, love or hate |
| Reddit, HN, community threads | Jargon, workarounds, what they recommend to each other | Technical, skeptical, a vocal minority |
| GitHub issues | Exact failure modes, workarounds | Power users, and it only shows problems |
| Support threads | Confusion and breakage | Problems only; the silent majority is missing |
| Job postings | The pain a company admits to, and its tool stack | Aspirational, not current reality |
| Survey, open-ended | Language at scale | Primed by how the question was framed |
| Survey, multiple choice | Rough proportions | The options you offered shape the answers |

## What to mine (Amy Hoy's Sales Safari)

- **Pains:** problems and workarounds they describe without being asked.
- **Jargon:** the exact words they use for the problem.
- **Recommendations:** what they tell each other to use or avoid.
- **Worldview:** how they see themselves and the problem.
