# Data map for the home-loan journey

One entry per stage: the objects, the minimum fields, where they live, and the quality trap to check first. Check the trap before building anything on the stage. As of Oct 2026.

- **Lead** (CRM, WhatsApp business provider, telephony). Fields: `lead_id`, `source` (builder, project, DSA, click-to-WhatsApp, organic), `project_id` and `unit_id` for APF, `consent_marketing` with its DLT consent reference, `consent_processing`, `language_pref`, `first_touch_ts`. Trap: duplicates across phone, email and project; dispositions typed as free text; `source` overwritten by the last touch.
- **Intent and qualification.** `purpose` (purchase, BT, top-up, LAP, self-construction), `property_stage` (APF-approved, under construction, ready, resale, plot), `timeline`, `budget`, `employment_type` (salaried, self-employed professional, self-employed business), `gross_income`, `co_applicant_present`, `city`, `existing_emis`, `bureau_score` (soft, consented). Trap: income captured in lakhs against rupees, and monthly against annual, in the same column.
- **Eligibility.** FOIR slab by income band (bank-specific, 20 to 70%), LTV cap by ticket (90, 80, 75%), property cost excluding stamp duty (except homes up to ₹10 lakh), tenor to retirement age, rate card by lender and bureau band, processing fee, lender exclusions (pin code, builder, profile). Output: in-principle amount per lender, EMI, APR per KFS. Trap: an in-principle treated as a sanction; a rate card left stale after a repo move (re-check everything dated before 7 Oct 2026).
- **Documents and KYC.** See `extraction-fields.md`. `kyc_mode` (Aadhaar OTP e-KYC, video KYC, CKYC fetch, DigiLocker), `kyc_ts`, Aadhaar XML or QR age (3 working days or fewer for video KYC). Trap: an account opened on OTP e-KYC alone is limited to one year without a video-KYC upgrade.
- **Bank data.** Source (AA `consent_id` with FIP, period and data life; or PDF with a tamper score), salary credits with employer match, average balance, bounces, existing EMIs, UPI merchant categories. Trap: the borrower's primary account sits on a different mobile number from the one used for AA; the cited reason for AA failures in affordable housing.
- **Credit assessment.** `credit_manager_id`, deviations list, personal-discussion notes as structured fields, fraud flags, credit appraisal memo fields. Trap: personal-discussion notes as free text. A large NBFC converted 48 lakh historical notes to structured data to fix this; cheaper to structure them on day one.
- **Sanction.** `sanction_letter_id`, the KFS object (APR, every fee, prepayment terms, cooling-off days, recovery policy), validity, conditions precedent, legal and technical status (skipped for APF units). Trap: KFS numbers drifting from the CRM's `rate` field. The KFS is the source; the CRM field is a cache.
- **Legal and technical.** For non-APF: title-chain years, valuer's value against agreement value, plan deviations. Trap: LTV computed on agreement value when the valuation is lower. The cap applies to the lower of the two.
- **Disbursal.** Tranche schedule, builder demand letter, own-contribution proof, pre-EMI start. Trap: an upfront disbursal request on an under-construction project, which is not permitted.
- **Servicing, BT, top-up.** Benchmark type (repo, MCLR, fixed, dual), spread, reset date, outstanding, `sanction_date`, prepayment history. Trap: loans sanctioned before 1 Jan 2026 may still carry prepayment charges by contract. Check `sanction_date` before telling anyone that switching is free.
- **Payout and economics (internal).** Lender, payout %, clawback window, processing fee collected against benchmark, disbursal date, payout received. Trap: payout computed on sanction rather than disbursal.
- **Conversations.** Every call and WhatsApp thread: transcript, language mix, intents, disposition, promised action, consent events, human hand-off flag, QC score. This is the asset nobody has. Build it first; every later model (lead scoring, BT propensity, agent-assist) reads from it.

Every write to any of these from an automated system carries an event row: actor, object, field, old value, new value, timestamp, the consent or approval it rests on.
