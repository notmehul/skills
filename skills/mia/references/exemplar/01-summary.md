# Northlake: market intelligence (fictional exemplar)

Pune, incorporated Feb 2024, 10 people, pre-revenue. Raising $500K at $4M post-money for 18-20 months of runway. Two products on one patent-applied substrate: Keel, a local-first AI memory layer with a free MIT core, and Ledgerline, a governed semantic layer over warehouses at $49-99 per user per month.


## Read

- One problem-anchored market: governed, provable AI access to what an organisation knows. TAM $7.8B in 2026 to $18.4B in 2030, ~24% a year. SAM $488M. India pool $73M.
- Only Ledgerline can earn against that today: $0.2-2.9M over three years at list. Standalone memory-layer spend is about $6M worldwide; Keel is selling into a budget line that must first be created.
- The pitch's moat does not hold. Recallio built the local-first agent-protocol memory server and retired it; Quillgraph raised $7.5M to build the offline engine; a lot of free projects match most of Keel's surface.
- The one unmatched feature is on Ledgerline, not Keel: cryptographically signed, re-verifiable answers. Nothing in 25 solutions does it.
- Traction is unverified: 168 package-index downloads a month against a claimed 16,000; no named customer, contract, benchmark or patent document.
- Regulation helps Ledgerline more than Keel. Data-protection duties from 13 May 2027 reward audit trails, which any vendor with a log can ship; they impose no residency mandate.
