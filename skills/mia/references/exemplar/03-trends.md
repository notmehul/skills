# Trends

Twelve forces that pass the company-specificity test. Five tailwinds, seven headwinds. Verdict: mixed. Full sources on request; every row is anchored to a claim in the run.


## Net assessment

| | |
|---|---|
| Dominant tailwind | Data-protection audit-trail duties from 13 May 2027, dated and aimed at the beachhead. Architecture-neutral: rewards provenance, not local-first. |
| Dominant headwind | Platform absorption, observed not forecast. It acts on whether the memory category is a purchasable budget line at all. |
| Why the headwind wins | A dated tailwind into a category that may not carry a budget line is worth less than an undated headwind against the budget line existing. |
| Tips to net tailwind | the IT ministry notification naming restricted data categories; Indian tenders naming residency for AI systems. |
| Tips to net headwind | A governed on-prem memory store from Vendor A, Vendor B or Vendor C; Recallio confirming Recallio Local died for lack of demand. |

## Ledgerline-side forces, carried separately

- Ledgerlinentic layer market $2.71B growing 23.3%, verified. Here the budget line exists and $49-99 per user lands inside it.
- Data-protection audit duties apply more directly to Ledgerline: append-only log, plan-time RBAC and signed evidence packs already ship.
- Absorption exists here too, with richer absorbers: Warehouse B and Warehouse A ship conversational analytics into the warehouse.

## Checked and dropped

> Failed the company-specificity test, not the space test.
- Enterprise AI budget growth ($37B in 2025): applies to every company in the sector.
- Falling inference cost: cuts both ways with equal force.
- Rupee cost base: real, but a standing condition; belongs in the funding analysis.
- Vector-database commoditisation (a a relational database extension): tier-3 sources only.
- agent-protocol server security incidents: could gate third-party agent-protocol servers entirely; no citable evidence yet.
