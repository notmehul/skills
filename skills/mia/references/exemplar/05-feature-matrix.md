## Read

- Keel's claimed moat, unretrofittable local-first sovereignty, scores full marks alongside two free projects. Recallio retrofitted it in 16 months and then retired it.
- Keel leads outright on one row only: deterministic, hash-stable citations. It scores zero on the relational graph the pitch leads with, on signing, on benchmarks and on certification.
- Ledgerline holds the single unmatched feature in the whole deal: cryptographically signed, re-verifiable answers. Nothing in either set does it.
