# Relationship Brief

Generate a tight, decision-useful brief on one person before an interaction. This
operationalizes Mehul's own relationship-intelligence thesis — model **relationship
state over time**, not CRM fields. (Recall-aware: see `marshy-alignment-engine`
and `key-collaborators` in `~/.marshmallow/`; `grep` the graph for the person.)

## Gather first

- Search `~/.marshmallow/` (graph + indexes) and any provided notes/threads for
  the person. For Biome people, `key-collaborators` is the source of record.
- If evidence is thin, say so — don't invent history. Ask for the missing piece
  or mark it unknown.

## The five primitives (output shape)

1. **Who they are to you** — role, how you know them, relationship tier, and the
   power/advocacy dynamic (ally, approver, gatekeeper, peer).
2. **Timeline / state** — last meaningful interaction; warming or cooling; trust
   level; reciprocity (who owes whom).
3. **Open loops** — promises made (either direction), unresolved asks, pending
   follow-ups. Also any "no" or "not now" they gave, with its date and the
   recheck window; a standing "no" overrides the next action until that window
   passes or they reopen it themselves.
4. **What changed** since last time — role moves, context shifts, new stakes.
5. **Next thoughtful action** — not just "follow up": the *specific* right move
   (send the intro you promised, congratulate the new role, raise scope, hold
   back and don't pitch, etc.).

## Principles

- **Thoughtful, not transactional.** The goal is to show up well, not to extract.
- **Private by default.** Sensitive impressions stay private; flag anything that
  shouldn't be said externally.
- Keep it scannable — a brief you can read in the elevator, not a dossier.

## Ask When

- The interaction is high-stakes and the evidence is stale or thin — confirm
  current state before relying on the brief.
