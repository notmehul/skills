# Measurement menu

Pick one primary DV and cap the secondaries. Use validated items unchanged; a 2025 review of trust scales says so in as many words. Always collect AI-correct and AI-wrong trials so over- and under-reliance are both estimable. Sources are as of Oct 2026.

| Construct | Instrument | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Usability | SUS (10 items); UMUX-Lite (2 items) | Stable. UMUX-Lite for repeated measures. |
| Workload | NASA-TLX, raw (unweighted), 6 subscales | Report subscales when the manipulation targets one. |
| Trust (self-report) | Jian 12-item, scored as two factors, trust and distrust (FAccT 2025, N=1,485); Trust in Automation short form S-TIAS, 3 items (Frontiers in AI, May 2025); MDMT v2, 20 items, 5 dimensions (2025); TILLMI, 6-item LLM trust (arXiv 2502.21028) | The 19-item Körber TiA did not replicate for generative AI in a 2026 N=2,169 adaptation (three factors emerged). A 2025 N=292 study suggests the two-item TiA for a general assessment. |
| Reliance (behavioural) | Agreement rate; switch rate (two-step paradigm); over-reliance = P(follow AI given AI wrong); under-reliance = P(reject AI given AI right); Appropriateness of Reliance pair (IUI 2023): RAIR = share of initially-wrong cases where the person switches to a correct AI answer, RSR = share of initially-right cases where the person resists a wrong AI answer (formulas UNVERIFIED against the full paper; abstract verified) | Report both error types. State the paradigm (one-step versus two-step); it changes over-reliance (2026 evidence). |
| Calibration | Pre and post confidence on 0 to 100; Brier score; calibration and discrimination gap | The 2025 design that compares a person's confidence in each LLM answer with the model's own confidence is reusable. |
| Delegation | Share of steps delegated; whether delegated output was inspected; edit distance to AI output | Log at step level. |
| Skill after-effect | Unaided performance in a post phase; comprehension quiz on just-completed work (2026); latent-ability change model (2026) | The after-effect is the finding reviewers now expect in offloading papers. |
| Creativity | Alternate Uses Test originality and fluency; Remote Associates Test; group-level diversity via embedding cosine distance with Monte Carlo sampling | Report individual and collective measures separately. |
| Companion use | UCLA loneliness; emotional dependence and problematic-use items from the 2025 RCT; usage intensity from logs | Intensity, not modality, carried the RCT effect. |
| Accessibility | Task-specific accuracy of model output rated against ground truth; perceived reliability 1 to 7; number of unreliable claims detected | Diary studies with blind and low-vision users: 2 weeks, N about 20, was the CHI 2026 norm. |
| Qualitative rigour | Two coders, kappa or Krippendorff's alpha on at least 20% of the corpus; evidence trail from code to excerpt; reflexivity note; LLM role disclosed | AI-to-human agreement must be compared with human-to-human agreement on the same codes. |

## LLM-assisted coding

Acceptable when the human owns the codebook, humans code a stated fraction with reported agreement, AI-to-human agreement is compared with human-to-human, the Methods section discloses it, and participant consent covers third-party processing. Unacceptable when the data come from vulnerable participants and the consent did not anticipate it, or when the LLM is the sole coder.
