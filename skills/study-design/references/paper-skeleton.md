# CHI-style paper skeleton

Short paper up to 5,000 words; Poster up to 6 pages, non-archival. Word counts per section are for the short paper. Length must match the contribution; a 12,000-word paper with a one-finding study is desk-rejected.

- **Title**: finding or system first, mechanism second.
- **Abstract (150 to 200 words)**: problem, method with N and design, two or three concrete findings with numbers, implication.
- **Introduction (600 to 800)**: the behaviour at stake; the gap in one paragraph; research questions; contributions as a bulleted list of 2 or 3 items.
- **Related work (700 to 900)**: three subsections at most, each ending with what is unresolved. Thin related work is CHI's most frequent assisted desk-reject reason; `research-brief` owns this section.
- **Method (900 to 1,200)**: participants (N, recruitment, pay, exclusions); design and counterbalancing; stimuli and model details (id, date, prompts); measures with instrument citations; procedure; analysis plan; preregistration link; ethics statement; data handling.
- **Results (900 to 1,200)**: primary test first with effect size, CI and a figure; then secondary; then exploratory, labelled; qualitative themes with counts and quotes.
- **Discussion (600 to 800)**: what changes for design and for theory; competing explanations; limitations stated specifically (sample, single session, task realism, model drift); future work you could do.
- **Conclusion (100 to 150)**.
- **Supplementary**: stimuli, prompts, analysis code, de-identified data, preregistration PDF, deviations log. The CHI reviewing guide says complete supplementary material should be rewarded.

## Writing that reads as research

Claims sized to the evidence; numbers with uncertainty; competing explanations named; limitations specific ("single-session, English-only, one model, 70/30 correctness ratio") rather than "small sample"; prior work engaged by finding, not by list; no adjective doing the work of data. Cite your own prior work in the third person; a reference marked "anonymous" is grounds for desk rejection. LLM-generated text beyond editing must be marked.

## Pieces that are not papers

There is no alt.chi track. A provocation goes to Posters or a workshop. A position paper with no data goes to a NeurIPS or ICLR human-AI workshop, which welcome "Opinion:" papers, rather than to CHI.
