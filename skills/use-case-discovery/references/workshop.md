# The 90-minute workshop

Run it after the shadowing and the interview, with the people who do the work in the room. The sponsor listens.

| Minutes | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| 0 to 10 | The shadowed workflow map on the wall. Corrections from the people who do it. |
| 10 to 30 | Each person writes their own "anti to-do list" silently on cards: repetitive work, waiting on others, stuck getting started. |
| 30 to 50 | Cluster the cards onto the map. Tag each cluster as repetitive, skill bottleneck, or ambiguity. |
| 50 to 70 | Classify each candidate (automate, assist, decision-support, leave human) by show of hands from the people who do the work, not from the sponsor. |
| 70 to 85 | For the top three: volume, how you would know it worked, who owns it. |
| 85 to 90 | The next step and its date. |

Leave with the corrected map, the clustered candidates with their classifications, and a named owner for each of the top three.
